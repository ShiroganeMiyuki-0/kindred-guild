⚔️ Getting Started

Welcome to Kindred Guild! Here's everything you need to know to start posting tasks, earning coins, and building your reputation in the community.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up with your email — no password needed. We'll send you a magic link. Click it and you're in. Pick a username that represents you in the Guild.

💡 Your username is how others will know you. Choose something memorable!
2

Explore the Quest Board

Browse available tasks posted by community members. Filter by type (free, coins, UPI), search by tags, or sort by deadline. Found something you can do? Accept it!

💡 Start with free tasks to build your reputation before taking on paid quests.
3

Post Your First Task

Need help with something? Post a quest! Use the quick templates (logo design, bug fix, tutoring, etc.) to get started fast. Choose your payment type and set a deadline.

💡 Free tasks have zero fees. Paid tasks use Fairy Coins (locked in escrow) or direct UPI.
4

Understand Fairy Coins

Fairy Coins are our internal currency. You earn them by completing tasks and spend them to post paid tasks. They're locked in escrow when you post — workers only get paid when you approve the work.

💡 Need more coins? Buy them via UPI on the Buy Coins page, or earn them by completing quests.
5

Build Your Reputation

Every completed task builds your reputation score. Both sides rate each other after completion (double-blind — neither sees the other's rating until both submit). Good reputation = more tasks.

💡 Reputation is visible on your profile. Higher scores unlock more quest opportunities.

Tips for Success

⭐

Be Specific

When posting a task, include all relevant details. The more specific you are, the better responses you'll get. Include examples, links, and clear requirements.

⏰

Set Realistic Deadlines

Give workers enough time to do quality work. If a quest expires, your coins are refunded automatically and you can re-post.

🛡️

Use Escrow for Safety

Paid tasks with Fairy Coins are protected by escrow. Your coins lock when you post and only release when you approve the work. If something goes wrong, you can file a dispute.

💬

Communicate

Use the messaging system to stay in touch with workers/posters. Share updates, ask questions, and be responsive. Good communication = better outcomes.
