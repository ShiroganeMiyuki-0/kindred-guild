## Reddit Posts

### r/SideProject
**Title:** I built a community task board where people help each other — free or paid, protected by escrow

**Body:**
Hey r/SideProject! I built Kindred Guild — a platform where communities can post tasks and get help from each other.

**How it works:**
- Post a task (free or paid with internal coins/UPI)
- Someone accepts it
- They submit proof of work
- You approve and payment releases
- Both sides rate each other

**Key features:**
- 🪙 Fairy Coins (internal currency) locked in escrow — no scams
- ⭐ Double-blind ratings — no retaliation
- 🏰 Guild Hall — community chat channels
- 🏆 Leaderboard — reputation-based rankings
- ⏰ Auto-approval after 48h — workers aren't left hanging
- 📋 Quick templates — post tasks in seconds

It's not just for coding — logo design, tutoring, errands, content writing, anything your community needs.

**Tech stack:** Vanilla HTML/JS, Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Realtime), Vercel, no frameworks.

Check it out: https://kindredguild.org

Would love feedback on the UX and concept!
