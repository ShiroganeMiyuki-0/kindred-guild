How Your Money is Protected

Every transaction on Kindred Guild follows a clear, auditable path:

When you post a quest with coins:
Your reward + 10% commission are locked immediately. This ensures you have the funds. The worker knows the money is real. When work is approved, coins release to the worker. If there's a dispute, both sides can appeal.
When you post a quest with UPI:
You pay the worker directly peer-to-peer. We only hold the 10% commission in coins as a safety deposit. This protects us from fraud while keeping your payment direct.
If something goes wrong:
Coins are refunded. UPI payments are documented. Disputes are resolved by reviewing proof of work. We don't take sides—we follow the evidence.

Who We Are

Kindred Guild is built and maintained by one person — no venture capital, no corporate board, no ad revenue pulling the incentives sideways. It exists because gig work shouldn't feel like a cold transaction with a faceless platform taking its cut and disappearing when something goes wrong.

The guild survives on three things, all disclosed upfront: a 10% commission on quests, optional donations from people who believe in the mission, and optional coin purchases. That's the whole model — nothing hidden behind it.

That structure isn't just ethics — it's self-interest that happens to align with yours. If this platform stops being fair, people leave, and it has nothing left to stand on. So trust here isn't a marketing line. It's the only thing keeping the lights on.

What Makes Us Different

Most gig platforms are designed to extract value from workers. Kindred Guild is designed to protect it:

No Algorithm Manipulation: Quests are sorted by date, deadline, or pay—not by what makes us the most money.
No Deactivation Without Reason: Your account won't be suspended arbitrarily. We follow clear rules.
No Data Selling: We don't sell your information. We don't track you across the web. Your data is yours.
No Forced Exclusivity: Work on Kindred Guild and other platforms simultaneously. We don't own you.
Reversible Actions: Made a mistake? You can undo it within 24 hours. Delete a quest. Edit a post. Restore a deleted item.

Our Commitment to Safety

We take safety seriously. Here's what we do:

  • Verification: Profiles are tied to real email addresses. Repeat offenders are tracked.
  • Reporting: Users can report scams, harassment, or inappropriate behavior.
  • Moderation: We review reports and take action against bad actors.
  • Transparency: Strikes and suspensions are logged. Users can see why they were penalized.
  • Privacy: Ratings are anonymous. You won't be harassed by someone you rated poorly.
