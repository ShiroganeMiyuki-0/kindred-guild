How to Play

🎮 Input Methods

Use webcam body tracking, keyboard, or mobile touch buttons. All three work simultaneously.

👊 Attacks

PUNCH — Snap fist forward (J key / tap PUNCH). KICK — Raise knee/ankle high (I key / tap KICK). Each has a cooldown after use.

🛡️ Defense

GUARD — Hold both hands up (K key / tap GUARD). Blocks all damage and opens a counter window. DUCK — Lower your hips (S key / tap DUCK) to dodge high attacks and supers.

⚡ Ki & Super

CHARGE — Bring hands together (L key / tap CHARGE) to build Ki energy. At 100%, use SUPER (Space / tap SUPER) to unleash a devastating beam!

🏆 Rounds

Best of 3 rounds. The AI adapts to your favorite moves — mix up your strategy! Win to face tougher opponents.
