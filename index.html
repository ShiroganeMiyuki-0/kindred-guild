How It Works

Three steps. That's it.

1
📝

Post a Task

Describe what you need. Set a reward (or make it free). Your coins stay locked in escrow until the job is done.

2
🤝

Someone Accepts

A community member picks up your task. They message you, share updates, and submit proof when done.

3
✅

Approve & Pay

Review the work. Approve it and the payment releases. Both sides rate each other. Done.

🪙 How Fairy Coins Work

Our internal currency. Think of it like gold in a game — earned, spent, and locked in escrow. Never leaves the platform.

💰

Earn

Complete tasks, get referrals, or receive gifts. Coins flow into your balance.

🔒

Lock

Post a paid quest and coins lock in escrow. They leave your balance but aren't given to anyone yet.

✅

Release

Approve the work and coins release to the worker. If you cancel, coins refund to you.

🔄

Repeat

Spend coins to post more tasks, or buy more via UPI. The cycle keeps the guild running.

💡 Good to know: Fairy Coins are not cryptocurrency. They can't be cashed out or transferred off-platform. They exist only inside Kindred Guild to keep the economy fair and scam-free.

Common Questions

Is this free to use?

Yes. You can post and accept free tasks with no cost. Paid tasks use Fairy Coins (our internal currency) or direct UPI payments. There's a 10% fee on paid tasks to keep the platform running.

What are Fairy Coins?

They're our internal currency. You earn them by completing tasks, and spend them to post paid tasks. You can also buy coins via UPI. They're not cryptocurrency — they stay inside the platform.

What if someone doesn't do the work?

Coins are held in escrow. If the worker doesn't deliver, you can request revisions or file a dispute. The poster can also cancel and get refunded if no work has started.

Can I edit my task after posting?

Yes. You can change the title, description, tags, payment type, and reward amount as long as the task is still open or accepted. You can also cancel and get a refund.

Can I invite friends?

Yes! Every user gets a unique referral code. Share it with friends to grow the community. Learn more.

Who runs this?

Kindred Guild is an independent project. We're a small team building tools for community cooperation. No venture capital, no corporate agenda. Check the Guild Charter for details.
