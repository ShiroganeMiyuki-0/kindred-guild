📜 Quest Rules & Guidelines

Everything you need to know before posting or accepting a quest. Read this once, avoid problems forever.

📝 For Quest Posters

  • 1️⃣ Be specific. A clear title and detailed description get better responses. Include what "done" looks like — deliverables, format, quality expectations.
  • 2️⃣ Set a realistic deadline. Don't give 24 hours for a week of work. Workers will skip quests with unreasonable timelines.
  • 3️⃣ Lock your coins upfront. For paid quests, coins are deducted when you post (reward + 10% commission). This shows workers you're serious.
  • 4️⃣ Respond within 48 hours when proof is submitted. If you don't, the quest auto-approves and the worker gets paid. This protects workers from ghosting.
  • 5️⃣ Request revisions before disputing. If the work isn't right, ask for changes first. Disputes should be a last resort.
  • 6️⃣ Free quests are commitments too. Even if no money is involved, treat free quests seriously. Abandoning free quests hurts your reputation.

🛡️ For Workers

  • 1️⃣ Only accept what you can deliver. Check the deadline, read the full description, and make sure you have the skills before accepting.
  • 2️⃣ Upload proof when you submit. Screenshots, documents, links, videos — anything that shows the work is done. At least 1 proof file is required.
  • 3️⃣ Communicate during the quest. Post updates in the coordination board. If you're stuck, say so early. Silence looks bad.
  • 4️⃣ Don't abandon accepted quests. If you can't finish, tell the poster ASAP. Abandoning quests without notice earns strikes.
  • 5️⃣ Rate honestly after completion. Double-blind ratings are revealed only after both sides submit. No retaliation risk.

🪙 Payments & Fairy Coins

  • Fairy Coins are the internal currency. You earn them by completing tasks, and spend them to post paid quests. Coins stay inside the platform — they cannot be cashed out.
  • Commission: A 10% fee is added on top of the reward (e.g., 100 FC quest costs 110 FC total). This keeps the guild running.
  • Escrow: Your coins lock when you post. They release to the worker only when you approve. If you cancel before work starts, you get a full refund.
  • UPI Direct: For real-money payments between users. We lock a 10% coin deposit as a trust deposit (like a security deposit). The actual UPI payment happens directly between users — the platform doesn't handle real money.
  • Free quests: No payment, no commission, no escrow. Trust comes from the reputation system.

⚠️ Disputes — How They Work

Disputes are for when things go wrong. Here's the process:

1. Worker submits proof

Upload at least 1 file showing the completed work. A 48-hour countdown starts.

2. Poster reviews (48 hours)

Approve to release payment, request revision, or file a dispute with a specific reason.

3. If dispute is filed

Coins stay locked. Both sides discuss in the coordination board. Either side can propose a resolution.

4. Resolution

Release to worker: Worker gets paid. Refund poster: Poster gets coins back. Both parties must agree, or the admin decides.

5. If no response in 48 hours

The quest auto-approves and the worker gets paid. This prevents posters from holding coins hostage.

📌 Dispute reasons: When filing a dispute, you must select a reason — work not delivered, quality unacceptable, scope changed, poster not responding, or other. This helps resolve things faster.

⏰ Auto-Refund Rules

These rules protect everyone from stale quests:

  • Open for 7+ days with no accept: Quest auto-cancels, poster gets a full coin refund.
  • Worker accepted but deadline passed with no submission: Quest auto-cancels, poster gets refunded, worker gets a strike.
  • Proof submitted, poster doesn't respond in 48 hours: Quest auto-approves, worker gets paid.
  • Dispute filed, no response in 72 hours: The responding side's proposal is auto-accepted.

⭐ Reputation System

  • After each completed quest (non-free), both sides rate each other from 1-5 stars.
  • Ratings are double-blind — hidden until both submit. No retaliation risk.
  • If one side doesn't rate within 7 days, ratings auto-reveal.
  • Your reputation score is the average of all revealed ratings, scaled to 0-50.
  • Higher reputation = more visibility on the quest board = more work opportunities.

🚨 Strikes & Suspensions

  • Abandoning quests without notice, failing to deliver, or spamming fake quests earns strikes.
  • 3 strikes = account suspended. You can't post or accept quests.
  • Strikes are reviewed by the admin. If you believe a strike was unfair, contact support.
