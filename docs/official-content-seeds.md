# Official Guild Content Workflow

Kindred Guild may publish platform-authored examples, onboarding quests, and official worker availability listings to keep a new community from opening to an empty board. These entries must be created from an administrator account and must carry the visible labels **Official Guild Listing** or **Official Guild Quest**. They must never impersonate ordinary members or be presented as organic community activity.

## Suggested launch content

| Area | Example title | Purpose | Required label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quest Board | Help us test the new task workflow | Gives new members a safe, low-stakes first quest and gathers usability feedback. | Official Guild Quest |
| Quest Board | Share a local skill you can teach | Demonstrates a free goodwill task and encourages worker participation. | Official Guild Quest |
| Workers | Guild onboarding helper | Explains how a new member can get help navigating the product. | Official Guild Listing |
| Workers | Accessibility feedback partner | Invites structured feedback on mobile layout and readable text. | Official Guild Listing |

## Grant and announcement workflow

When an administrator grants a wish through the existing admin action, the wish page displays the latest granted wish and the Quest Board displays a database-backed announcement linking members to the completed wish. The announcement is generated from the real `wishes` record and is not a fabricated activity event.

For future automation, use an admin-approved content queue with audit fields such as author, approval time, publication time, and content source. Automated publication should be opt-in, rate-limited, and visibly attributed to the Guild system.
