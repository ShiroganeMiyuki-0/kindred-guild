## Messaging rule

The first public message should lead with the human idea and the next action. Technical details such as escrow mechanics, internal currency, database behavior, and live counters should stay on dedicated explanation pages or appear only after the visitor chooses to learn more. Honest early-stage language is better than inflated numbers: say that the Guild is in its founding season rather than presenting a quiet board as if it were a large established network.
