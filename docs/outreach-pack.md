# Kindred Guild outreach pack

## Positioning

Kindred Guild is a member-shaped place for useful odd jobs, shared trust, and community wishes. Someone can bring a real task, offer a skill, help a neighbor, or propose what the Guild should build next. The invitation is not to join another noisy platform; it is to help shape a small world that becomes more useful through the people who participate.

## Short post

**Bring one useful thing to the Guild.**

Need a hand with something practical? Have a skill to offer? Want to suggest what a community should build next? Kindred Guild is a small member-shaped marketplace for quests, workers, messages, trust, and shared wishes.

Start with one honest request, one useful offer, or one idea for the Guild’s next chapter: [kindredguild.org](https://kindredguild.org/)

## Longer post

Most platforms ask you to consume. Kindred Guild asks what would actually help.

Post a free or paid quest. Offer a skill. Find a person who can help. Coordinate in the Guild. Work toward a clear outcome. Then let the people who participate shape what comes next through Fairy Wishes.

It is early, which means your first useful contribution can still matter. No invented activity, no borrowed characters, and no promise that a number will make your life better—just a small place for people to help one another and decide what should exist next.

Visit [kindredguild.org](https://kindredguild.org/) and bring one real thing.

## Founding-season invitation

The Guild is in its founding season. If you join now, you are not entering a finished product with a fixed roadmap. You can bring a request, offer a skill, meet a useful person, or help decide what the community should build next.

## Visual direction

Use an original guild noticeboard, warm lantern light, hand-pinned quest cards, diverse anonymous silhouettes, and a subtle frontier-town atmosphere. Do not use protected anime characters, character names, recognizable quotes, screenshots, logos, or imitation costumes. The tone can be warm, odd-job-oriented, and adventurous without borrowing a copyrighted character’s identity.

## Posting discipline

Only publish quests, member stories, or outcomes that are real and approved for sharing. Do not fabricate bots, testimonials, completed wishes, member counts, earnings, or urgency. If the Guild is quiet, say that it is early and invite one concrete contribution.
