# Separate launch advertising campaigns

## Kindred Guild

### Positioning

Kindred Guild is a member-shaped online guild where people can post tasks, offer skills, find help, meet other members, and influence what the community builds next. The first campaign should sell belonging and usefulness rather than coins or abstract governance.

### Primary audience

The first audience is people in online communities who regularly ask for recommendations, small favors, design help, study help, technical help, or local assistance. A second audience is people with useful skills who want a low-pressure way to help others and become known for reliable contributions.

### Image-ad headline

**A guild shaped by its members.**

Supporting copy: **Post a quest. Find help. Shape what comes next.**

Call to action: **Join the Founding Season**.

### Eight-second vertical video script

**0:00–0:02:** A quiet lantern-lit quest board. On-screen text: “Need help with something?”

**0:02–0:04:** Three quest cards appear: “Post a Quest,” “Find Help,” and “Offer a Skill.”

**0:04–0:06:** The scene widens to show the Guild Hall and a small group of members. On-screen text: “A community you help shape.”

**0:06–0:08:** Gold title card. Voiceover: “Kindred Guild is a place to ask for help, offer what you know, and help shape what comes next.” On-screen CTA: “Join the Founding Season.”

### Safe claims

Use “free or paid quests,” “escrow for paid quests,” and “member-shaped community” only when the destination page explains the details clearly. Do not use fake member counts, fake testimonials, guaranteed earnings, or claims that the Guild already has a large active community.

## Anime Motion Fighter

### Positioning

Anime Motion Fighter is a simple browser arena prototype where players can use a camera or keyboard to move, block, and score. The first campaign should sell immediacy and playfulness, not a large feature list.

### Primary audience

The first audience is friends, families, anime fans, and casual players who enjoy quick browser experiments and physical party-game interactions. A second audience is developers and creators who enjoy trying camera-based game mechanics.

### Image-ad headline

**Move. Block. Score.**

Supporting copy: **Play with your camera or keyboard.**

Call to action: **Start a match in your browser**.

### Eight-second vertical video script

**0:00–0:02:** Two color-coded silhouettes raise their hands. On-screen text: “Your body is the controller.”

**0:02–0:04:** A hand reaches a torso target; a clear “HIT +1” response appears. A hand-to-hand contact shows “BLOCK.”

**0:04–0:06:** A foot action shows “KICK +2.” The camera frame and keyboard fallback appear side by side.

**0:06–0:08:** Blue and red score cards face off. Voiceover: “Move, block, and score in your browser.” On-screen CTA: “Start a match.”

### Safe claims

Use “camera or keyboard,” “hand and foot contact,” and “browser match.” Do not claim online multiplayer, professional motion capture, advanced AI, or perfect tracking until those features are genuinely reliable.

## Campaign rule

Keep the products separate in every advertisement. Kindred Guild is about belonging, usefulness, and member agency. Anime Motion Fighter is about a short, understandable arena activity. Cross-link them only after each product can explain itself in one sentence.

## Measurement

Use separate links or campaign parameters for each product and measure landing-page visits, first meaningful action, and return visits. For Kindred Guild, the first meaningful actions are Browse Tasks, Post a Task, or Offer Your Skills. For Anime Motion Fighter, the first meaningful action is entering fighter selection and starting a match. Do not optimize for clicks alone.
