# Kindred Guild social publishing pack

## Launch post

Kindred Guild is open for its founding season.

It is a new online guild where people can post a quest, offer a skill, find help, and help shape what the community becomes. The Guild is still small, and that is part of the invitation: early members will help create the culture.

Explore the Guild: https://kindredguild.org/

## Founder story post

I built Kindred Guild as a place where asking for help does not have to feel transactional and offering help can become visible, trusted, and meaningful.

It combines a quest board for useful work, a Guild Hall for community, and Fairy Wishes for member-led decisions about what should happen next.

It is early. I am building it carefully and learning from the people who try it.

https://kindredguild.org/

## Official Guild quest post

**OFFICIAL GUILD QUEST — Help us improve the founding experience**

The Guild is looking for thoughtful early members to try the homepage, Quest Board, and Guild Hall and report one thing that feels confusing or one thing that feels welcoming.

This is an official administrator-authored onboarding quest. It is not a fake member post. If a reward is attached, publish the real reward and completion terms through the Quest Board before sharing this announcement.

## Publishing rule

Every automated or administrator-authored post must be labeled as official Guild content. Do not create undisclosed fake users, fake engagement, fake testimonials, or fabricated quest completions. Publish only claims that are true on the linked page.

## Suggested sequence

Publish the launch post first, then the founder story after a few days, then the worker invitation. Publish the official onboarding quest only after its actual Quest Board entry exists and its reward and completion terms are visible.
