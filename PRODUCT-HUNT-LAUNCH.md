# Product Hunt Launch — Kindred Guild 2.0

## Tagline
A free community task board where people help each other — like a real-life guild

## Description (for the PH listing)

**Last time we launched, Kindred Guild was a simple task board. Post a quest. Someone accepts. Done.**

**Now it's a full community.** Here's what changed:

### What's New Since v1

🛡️ **Escrow Protection** — Coins lock when you post. They only release when you approve the work. Neither side can run with the funds.

💬 **Guild Hall** — Real-time community chat with channels. Not a forum. Not a Discord. Built into the platform.

✨ **Fairy Wishes** — The community votes on what gets built next. Back wishes with coins or UPI. Your voice actually matters.

👥 **Friends & Groups** — Connect with people you've worked with. Form groups. Build your network inside the guild.

💬 **Direct Messaging** — Task-based conversations. Share updates, submit proof, resolve issues — all in one place.

🏆 **Leaderboard & Reputation** — Every completed task builds your score. Good workers get more work. Good posters get more responses.

🎁 **Referral System** — Invite friends, both earn 25 Fairy Coins. The guild grows by word of mouth.

🛡️ **Adventurers for Hire** — Post your skills and availability. Get hired by community members. No bidding wars.

🔍 **Find People** — Search for members by skill, name, or reputation. Connect with the right person for the job.

📜 **Activity Feed** — See what's happening across the guild in real time. New quests, completions, disputes — all transparent.

⏰ **Auto-Approval** — If a poster doesn't respond in 48 hours, the task auto-approves. Workers aren't left hanging.

👁️ **Double-Blind Ratings** — Both sides rate each other. Ratings are hidden until both submit. No retaliation.

🔄 **Undo History** — Made a mistake? View and restore cancelled or deleted tasks within a grace window.

🪙 **Buy Coins via UPI** — Indian users can purchase Fairy Coins directly through UPI. PayPal available too.

📱 **PWA Support** — Install it as an app on your phone. Works offline. Feels native.

📖 **Blog & Guides** — SEO-optimized content explaining how the platform works, comparisons with Fiverr/Upwork, and community education.

🛡️ **Admin Dashboard** — Full moderation tools. Suspend users, resolve disputes, manage quests, track platform health.

📜 **Guild Charter & Rules** — Clear terms, quest rules, and privacy policy. No hidden clauses.

### The Philosophy

Kindred Guild isn't a freelance marketplace. It's not Fiverr with a coat of paint. It's built around one idea:

**Communities can help each other if you give them the right tools.**

- Free tasks cost nothing. No fees, no catches.
- Paid tasks use Fairy Coins — an internal currency that never leaves the platform. Not crypto. Can't be cashed out.
- 10% fee on paid tasks to keep the lights on.
- Escrow protects both sides. Disputes are handled fairly.
- The guild theme isn't decoration — it changes how people treat each other.

### Who It's For

- Communities that need a better way to organize help
- Freelancers tired of Fiverr's race to the bottom
- People who want to earn by helping their neighbors
- Anyone building something and needing affordable help
- Mutual aid groups wanting structure without corporate overhead

### Tech Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS + Supabase (auth, database, real-time) + Vercel (hosting). No framework bloat. Loads fast. Works everywhere.

## First Comment (maker comment)

When we first launched, Kindred Guild was just a quest board — post a task, someone accepts it. Simple.

But the community asked for more. So we built more.

**The biggest change: trust infrastructure.**

We added escrow protection so nobody loses money on a bad deal. Double-blind ratings so both sides can be honest. Auto-approval so workers don't get stuck waiting. Dispute resolution for when things go wrong.

**The second biggest change: community tools.**

Guild Hall for real-time chat. Friends & Groups for networking. Fairy Wishes so the community decides what we build next. A leaderboard that rewards people who contribute.

**What hasn't changed:** It's still free to post and accept free tasks. Fairy Coins are still internal-only — not crypto, can't be cashed out. The guild theme still makes people treat each other like teammates instead of transactions.

We're not trying to replace Fiverr. We're building something for communities that want to help each other with structure and accountability.

Try it: https://kindredguild.org

Would love feedback — what's missing? What would make your community use this?

## Maker Story (for the "Story" section)

I built Kindred Guild because I was tired of watching the same pattern:

Someone posts "can anyone help me with X?" on social media. It gets 3 likes and disappears. Meanwhile, someone else nearby has the exact skill needed but never sees it.

The existing options were all wrong:
- **Fiverr/Upwork** — Race to the bottom. 20% fees. No community.
- **Facebook groups** — Chaotic. No accountability. Messages get buried.
- **WhatsApp groups** — Worse. No structure at all.

I wanted something in between — structured enough to hold people accountable, but community-oriented enough to feel like helping a neighbor, not hiring a contractor.

The guild theme came from a simple observation: in games, guilds work. People cooperate, help each other, build reputation. Why can't that work in real life?

So I built it. First version was just post-and-accept. Now it has escrow, chat, ratings, referrals, leaderboards, and a community voting system. 34 pages. Full platform.

It's free. It'll stay free for basic use. Fairy Coins are the internal currency — you earn them by helping, spend them to get help. Not crypto. Can't leave the platform.

The goal isn't to build a startup. It's to build a tool that makes communities stronger.
