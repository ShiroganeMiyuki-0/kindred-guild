GESTURE & ACTION GUIDE

PUNCH (Key: J / 1)
Snap either fist forward and extend the elbow — a fully-bent arm won't register.
HIGH KICK (Key: I / 2)
Raise knee or ankle high above hip level with speed.
DUCK / CROUCH (Key: S / Down)
Lower hips / squat down to evade high punches & beams.
GUARD / SHIELD (Key: K / 3)
Hold both hands close in front of face/head.
KI CHARGE (Key: L / 4)
Bring both wrists together near abdomen to accumulate Ki.
ANIME SUPER BEAM (Key: Space / 0)
At 100% Ki, thrust both palms forward to unleash beam!
