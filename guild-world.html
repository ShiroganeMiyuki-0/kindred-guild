Founding Season

A small community can still feel alive when its members can see continuity: what was done, who helped, what was decided, and what is waiting for the next person.

The first customs are being written now.

Kindred Guild is built by one person today, but it is not meant to be shaped by one person forever. Early members have an unusual responsibility: their kindness, humor, standards, and wishes become the first traditions of the Guild.

Arrive with curiosity. Help someone. Leave a trace of kindness. Invite the next person in.
